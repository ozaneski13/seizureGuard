# Datasets, Models & Methods Survey (August 2026)

Research sweep for seizureGuard: what appeared since the project started (January 2026), plus established resources the prototype missed. Every entry was verified live via search/fetch in August 2026. Items released after January 2026 are marked **[NEW since project start]**.

**Headline (honest):** as of August 2026 there is still **no public dataset of dog seizures on video**, and no published paper or open-source project doing camera-based seizure detection in pet dogs. seizureGuard's exact niche remains open. The closest data is rodent seizure video; the closest commercial product is Furbo's new camera-based "Seizure Alert" (no published accuracy). Everything below is one hop away — and much of it is directly usable.

## Datasets

- **RodEpil** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10431), Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17601357) — dataset, Nov 2025, open (CC BY 4.0) — 13,053 ~10s clips (2,952 seizure-positive) from 19 rodents, fixed cage cameras; TimeSformer baseline hits F1 97% with subject-wise CV. The only openly downloadable animal seizure video dataset; best pretraining/transfer source for a dog model.
- **K9-Bench** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02680), [project page](https://ogmenrobotics.github.io/K9Bench)) — dataset/benchmark, Jul 2026, open (CC BY 4.0) — **[NEW since project start]** 907 dog videos, ~5,000 QA pairs testing MLLM understanding of canine posture/actions. Directly quantifies seizureGuard's core risk: frontier MLLMs show limited zero-shot performance on canine motion reasoning — test your VLM here before trusting its confidence.
- **SmartHome-Bench** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12992), [code](https://github.com/Xinyi-0724/SmartHome-Bench-LLM)) — dataset, Jun 2025, open — 1,203 smart-home camera videos incl. pet distress/erratic-movement categories; their taxonomy+reflection prompting chain (TRLC) adds +11.6% accuracy. Same deployment setting and tooling as seizureGuard; usable eval clips and a portable prompting recipe.
- **Ultralytics Dog-Pose** ([docs](https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/pose/dog-pose/)) — dataset, 2024, open (AGPL-3.0 tooling) — 8,476 images, 24 keypoints per dog, plug-and-play with YOLO pose models. Fastest route to an on-device dog keypoint detector.
- **Animal Kingdom** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08129)) — dataset, CVPR 2022, open for research — 30K video sequences, 140 action classes, 850+ species incl. dogs. Best established animal-action pretraining source for action-aware embeddings (falling, shaking, struggling).
- **DogMo** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.24117v1)) — dataset, Oct 2025, paper open / dataset per project page — large multi-view RGB-D canine motion capture. Source of realistic normal dog movement for hard-negative mining (vigorous play vs seizure).
- **CREMD** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15349)) — dataset, Feb 2026, per paper — **[NEW since project start]** 923 annotated dog video clips (owners + professionals). Normal-behavior negatives, plus evidence on how noisy lay vs expert dog-behavior labels are.
- **Seizure-Semiology-Suite (S3)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21852)) — dataset/benchmark, May 2026 (ICML 2026 Spotlight), access terms unclear (clinical human video, likely on-request) — **[NEW since project start]** 438 human seizure videos, >35,000 dense semiology annotations, 7-task MLLM benchmark. Its recipe (concept-grounded pipeline, F1 0.96) matters even if the data stays gated.
- **NeuroMM-2026** ([HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NeuroMM/NeuroMM-2026)) — dataset, 2026 challenge, gated (CC BY-NC, manual approval) — **[NEW since project start]** 25,426 human samples: EEG + pre-computed video features from 7 vision backbones (raw video not included). Useful for training a lightweight classifier on frozen video embeddings — the local-model architecture seizureGuard could adopt.
- **Movement Sensor Dataset for Dog Behavior Classification** ([Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/vxhx934tbn/4)) — dataset, 2022, open — 45 dogs, 100 Hz IMU, 7 behaviors annotated from synced video. Best open baseline of normal dog motion dynamics for false-positive suppression.
- **AI-Hub pet behavior video corpus** ([AI-Hub Korea](https://aihub.or.kr/aihubdata/data/view.do?currMenu=115&topMenu=100&aihubDataSe=realm&dataSetSn=59)) — dataset, ~2021-2022, free but registration and downloads generally restricted to Korea — 514 hours of dog/cat behavior video, ~7.5M frames with boxes and 15 keypoints. The largest annotated pet-behavior corpus in existence, if the access hurdle can be cleared.
- **Kaggle canine intracranial EEG** ([Seizure Detection Challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/seizure-detection/data), [Pennsieve](https://discover.pennsieve.io/datasets/3/version/1)) — dataset, 2014, open — ~348 real dog seizures of iEEG (no video). Wrong modality, but the only public real dog-seizure data; useful for seizure timing/duration statistics that inform the 60s window and burst sampling.
- **GESTURES** ([GitHub, archived](https://github.com/fepegar/gestures-miccai-2021)) — dataset (features), 2021, open (raw videos withheld) — STCNN features from 183 human seizures; pretrained backbone + tiny RNN reaches 98.9%. Evidence that a cheap local classifier on pretrained features beats bespoke models.
- **MammAlps** ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/15441330), [code](https://github.com/eceo-epfl/MammAlps)) — dataset, CVPR 2025, open — event-triggered camera-trap behavior benchmark with false-trigger handling. A design template for exactly seizureGuard's capture pipeline (no dogs).
- **WildDog-Videos** ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21083480)) — dataset, Jul 2026, currently restricted (under review) — **[NEW since project start]** canid ethogram video with 161K frame-level annotations. Watch for public release.
- **DogFLW** ([GitHub](https://github.com/martvelge/DogFLW)) — dataset, 2024-2025, CC BY-NC on request — 4,335 dog face images, 46 landmarks. Only relevant if expanding to facial signs of focal seizures.

## Models & Tools

- **SuperAnimal-Quadruped (DeepLabCut Model Zoo)** ([paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-48792-2), [weights](https://huggingface.co/mwmathis/DeepLabCutModelZoo-SuperAnimal-Quadruped), [docs](https://deeplabcut.github.io/DeepLabCut/docs/ModelZoo.html)) — model, 2024, open weights — zero-shot 39-keypoint pose for dogs, ~3-line video inference, self-supervised video adaptation to cut jitter. The single strongest missed resource: free dog skeletons from the existing camera feed, enabling every pose-based method below.
- **Ultralytics YOLO26 + yolo26-pose** ([docs](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo26), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25164)) — model, public launch Jan 2026, AGPL-3.0 — **[NEW since project start]** NMS-free, up to 43% faster CPU inference, RLE keypoints stable under occlusion. Makes always-on local dog pose tracking on a CPU-only home box practical (fine-tune on Dog-Pose above).
- **gpt-5-nano / gpt-5-mini / gpt-5.4-mini** ([gpt-5-nano](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-5-nano), [gpt-5.4-mini](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-5.4-mini)) — product, Aug 2025 / Mar 2026, commercial API — gpt-5-nano ($0.05/$0.40 per 1M tokens) is ~8x cheaper on input than gpt-4.1-mini with vision support; gpt-5.4-mini (Mar 2026, **[NEW since project start]**) is the stronger-but-pricier option. A nano-screen + mini-confirm tier swap is the immediate cost win.
- **Qwen3-VL (2B-32B)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-VL)) — model, Sep-Nov 2025, Apache-2.0, Ollama-ready — video-native open VLM; 2B/4B run on an 8GB consumer GPU. The leading open-weights replacement for the paid OpenAI call.
- **SmolVLM2 (256M-2.2B)** ([HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM2-2.2B-Instruct)) — model, Feb 2025, Apache-2.0 — video QA in ~1.8 GB GPU RAM; Pi/Jetson-class feasible. Cheapest possible on-device "abnormal motor event" screener, reserving cloud calls for low-confidence cases.
- **Frigate NVR 0.15-0.18** ([docs](https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/genai/genai_objects/)) — tool, 2025-2026 (0.18 **[NEW since project start]**), open source — mature local pipeline of exactly seizureGuard's shape: motion regions -> tracking -> event frames -> vision LLM -> semantic search over events. Adopt as capture layer or steal its patterns (motion masks, event retention).
- **OpenAI logprobs recipe** ([Cookbook](https://cookbook.openai.com/examples/using_logprobs)) — guide, established — request logprobs on the yes/no token and threshold on the token probability. Probably the highest value-per-line change available to the current pipeline.
- **OpenAI Structured Outputs** ([docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs)) — guide, established, vision-compatible — enforce `{abnormal_event, observed_signs[], body_region, ...}` so parsing never breaks and the model must commit to auditable evidence.
- **AKS: Adaptive Keyframe Sampling** ([GitHub](https://github.com/ncTimTang/AKS)) — tool, CVPR 2025, open code — cheap scorer picks a fixed-size frame set maximizing prompt-relevance x temporal coverage before the expensive VLM call. Formalizes and improves the 2fps+burst heuristic.
- **Animal-CLIP** ([GitHub](https://github.com/PRIS-CV/Animal-CLIP)) — model, 2024, MIT with released weights — animal-specialized video action recognition (trained on Animal Kingdom, MammalNet). A middle layer between frame differencing and the VLM call.
- **ASBAR** ([GitHub](https://github.com/MitchFuchs/asbar)) — tool, 2023-2024, MIT — DeepLabCut pose -> PoseConv3D skeleton action classifier. A worked open-source template of the architecture seizureGuard should evolve toward (needs retraining for dogs).
- **Moondream 3 Preview** ([HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/moondream/moondream3-preview)) — model, late 2025, BSL 1.1 (personal use fine) — grounded detection/pointing VLM (image-only, CUDA needed). Could localize the dog and limbs per frame for explainable evidence.
- **VideoLLaMA3** ([GitHub](https://github.com/damo-nlp-sg/videollama3)) — model, Jan 2025, code Apache-2.0 but weights research-preview — backup video-native local model if Qwen3-VL underperforms; check the weight license first.
- **Video VLM frame-sampling cost guide** ([Forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/ai-for-video-engineering/articles-ai/video-vlms-frame-sampling-token-streaming-2026)) — guide, 2026 — concrete token math (~258 tokens/frame default vs ~66 low-res, ~75% cost cut) and the "set fps by the shortest event you must catch" sizing rule.

## Papers & Methods

- **Automated Seizure Classification Using Multimodal LLMs (UCLA)** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12632642/)) — paper, Oct 2025, open — the direct architectural sibling of seizureGuard: frame batches into a VLM. Result: VLM-only semiology features reach F1 0.76 vs 0.97 with expert features; large movements detected reliably, subtle ones missed; audio helps. Sets realistic expectations for the current design.
- **CDSD: Neurosymbolic Concept-Driven Skeleton-Based Seizure Detection** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21252), [code](https://github.com/Mr-TalhaIlyas/CDSD/)) — paper, Jun 2026 (MICCAI 2026), open code + pose data — **[NEW since project start]** pose -> motor-semiology concepts -> differentiable logic rules; ~90% sensitivity at 0.06 false detections/h. The most reusable pipeline found; maps cleanly onto SuperAnimal-Quadruped + canine semiology rules.
- **Forecasting Epileptic Seizures from Contactless Camera via Cross-Species Transfer** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12887)) — paper, Mar 2026, open (no code yet) — **[NEW since project start]** rodent-pretrained, few-shot fine-tuned video-only forecasting (>70% acc, 3-10s pre-ictal windows). Validates the exact premise of a RodEpil-pretrained dog model, in exactly seizureGuard's data regime (few labeled events per animal).
- **Learning Cross-Joint Attention for Generalizable Video-Based Seizure Detection** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23757)) — paper, Mar 2026, open — **[NEW since project start]** joint-dynamics attention beats raw-pixel models on unseen subjects. The evidence that pose dynamics, not appearance, is what generalizes across dogs/breeds.
- **VERA: Explainable VAD via Verbalized Learning of VLMs** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01095), [code](https://github.com/vera-framework/VERA)) — paper, CVPR 2025, open — replaces one "is this anomalous?" prompt with a small set of learned guiding questions, optimized against coarsely labeled clips, API-model compatible. The strongest prompt-side upgrade for a no-training-budget prototype.
- **AnomalyRuler** ([GitHub](https://github.com/Yuchen413/AnomalyRuler)) — paper/code, ECCV 2024, MIT — induce rules of *normality* from few-shot normal frames, then check events against them with temporal smoothing and a re-check step; ships an OpenAI API script. Transfers almost verbatim: induce this specific dog's normality rules from its own footage.
- **No Need For Real Anomaly: MLLM Zero-Shot VAD** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19248)) — paper, Feb 2026 (CVPR 2026), open — **[NEW since project start]** SOTA zero-shot VAD trained only on synthetic anomalies. Transferable idea: validate the prompt/threshold pipeline on staged or augmented abnormal-motion clips instead of waiting for real seizures.
- **Evaluation of Vision-LLMs in Surveillance Video** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23190)) — paper, Oct 2025, open — VLMs handle spatially salient events well (convulsive seizures qualify), struggle with subtle ones; few-shot exemplar images in the prompt sometimes *increased* false positives — cheap to A/B before adding example frames.
- **DUAL-VAD: Anomaly-Focused Sampling for VAD** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11605)) — paper, Sep 2025, open — softmax-based frame allocation concentrating budget on anomaly-dense segments. Independent validation of seizureGuard's motion-peak burst-sampling bet, with a more principled allocation scheme.
- **ConfBench** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01792)) — paper, Aug 2026, open — **[NEW since project start]** calibration benchmark showing VLM verbalized confidence is poorly calibrated (document-extraction domain, but consistent with 2025-2026 findings that token logprobs are more robust). Evidence base for distrusting the current verbalized "confidence" field.
- **VSViG: Real-time Skeleton-Based Seizure Detection** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.14775)) — paper, ECCV 2024, open preprint — pose patches -> spatiotemporal graph -> per-frame seizure probability, real-time. The method template for a cheap local pre-filter that gates VLM calls.
- **Deep Learning Approaches for Seizure Video Analysis (review)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10930)) — paper, 2024, open — field-defining survey of vision-based seizure detection: architectures, datasets, and the subject-wise-split evaluation discipline seizureGuard's testing should adopt.
- **Automated video seizure detection review (Frontiers Neuroinformatics)** ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroinformatics/articles/10.3389/fninf.2024.1324981/full)) — paper, 2024, open — maps clinically validated motion features, notably oscillation-frequency analysis of movement — computable from the existing OpenCV frame-differencing output at near-zero cost.
- **EPIDetect (mouse convulsion detection)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20614)) — paper, May 2024, open — camera-only convulsion detection in home-cage mice; read for how they handle grooming/scratching/sleep-twitch confounds — exactly seizureGuard's false-positive classes.
- **Epi-Moni: in-hospital canine accelerometer seizure detection** ([Frontiers Vet Sci](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/veterinary-science/articles/10.3389/fvets.2025.1558274/full)) — paper, Apr 2025, open — 12 dogs, 35 GTCS: sensitivity 74.3%, PPV 92.6%, 0.018 false alarms/day, 11s median latency. The published benchmark seizureGuard should compare itself against.
- **Munana et al.: Whistle collar evaluation** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7255659/)) — paper, 2020, open — a mainstream consumer collar caught only 18.6-22.1% of seizures in a 6-month home trial. The cautionary result that motivates camera-based monitoring as a complement.
- **Canine seizure semiology survey (Matz et al., JVIM)** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081834/)) — paper, 2025, open — structured canine seizure/dyskinesia semiology instrument. The best available vocabulary for upgrading the VLM prompt to specific, checkable canine signs.
- **Inter-observer agreement on canine paroxysmal event videos** ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4337258/)) — paper, 2015, open — even veterinary specialists show limited agreement classifying dog events from video alone. Any label pipeline (including a VLM's) inherits this noise floor; validate against clinician-adjudicated events.
- **IMU collar detecting seizure-alert dog behaviors** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12607429/)) — paper, Oct 2025, open — 92% accuracy in leave-one-dog-out validation; LODO cross-dog validation is the credibility standard to adopt.
- **Privacy-centric seizure detection (pose/masks/surface normals)** ([ACM/Springer](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1007/978-981-95-4972-6_25)) — paper, 2025, paywalled — detection survives when raw pixels are replaced by pose and masks: supports a local-only privacy mode with no footage sent to the cloud.
- **Universal Action Space** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09518), [code](https://github.com/franktpmvu/Universal-Action-Space)) — paper, Feb 2026, open code — **[NEW since project start]** cross-species action embeddings addressing biped-to-quadruped transfer; candidate feature extractor, but inspect dog-specific coverage before committing.
- **Wireless ambulatory video-EEG in unsedated dogs (James et al.)** ([JVIM](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jvim.14789)) — paper, 2017 (2024 standardization follow-up), open — video-EEG is the veterinary gold standard any camera system's labels are ultimately judged against; the Guelph group is the best data-sharing contact for verified seizure video.

## Commercial / Registry Landscape

- **Furbo Seizure Alert (Tomofun)** ([help center](https://help.furbo.com/hc/en-us/articles/48059227303321-Seizure-Alert)) — product, launch date unconfirmed (likely 2025-2026), commercial subscription — camera-based AI "seizure-like activity" alerts with a two-detections-in-5-minutes debounce and auto-tagged Pet Emergency category. The closest commercial analog to seizureGuard; publishes no accuracy numbers (page bot-blocks fetches; details verified via search snippets), which is a gap an honest hobby project can differentiate on.
- **PetPace Epilepsy Insights** ([announcement](https://petpace.com/tracking-dog-epilepsy-seizures-when-you-cant-be-there/), [Bio-Response program](https://petpace.com/researchers-leverage-petpace-smart-collar-in-groundbreaking-canine-epilepsy-study/)) — product, beta Aug 2025, commercial — collar-biometrics seizure logging with ~1h alert latency, gated on formal epilepsy diagnosis, built on a proprietary canine epilepsy biometric database (collaboration contact only, not open). The latency gap vs a 60s camera pipeline is notable.
- **PetVetAI / Pet Madness** ([site](https://petvetai.ai/)) — product, Jun 2026 PR, commercial — **[NEW since project start]** camera/smartphone AI health observation platform whose founding story cites undetected seizures — but the live site shows no explicit seizure detector and the .com domain is parked. Watch, treat as unproven.
- **AltumView Sentinare VLM fall detection** ([announcement](https://www.altumview.ca/2025/04/15/altumview-launched-first-fall-detection-feature-powered-by-large-vision-language-ai-model-vlm/)) — product, Apr 2025, commercial — shipping elder-care product: cheap local pose sensing proposes, a VLM confirms, claiming near-elimination of false alarms. Production proof of the two-stage architecture seizureGuard should converge to.
- **RVC Canine Epilepsy Research + Pet Epilepsy Tracker** ([RVC](https://www.rvc.ac.uk/research/animals-in-research/case-studies/canine-epilepsy-research)) — registry, ongoing, on-request — Europe's main owner seizure registry. Their finding that vets show low inter-rater agreement on owner seizure videos is directly load-bearing for label quality.
- **AKC Canine Health Foundation epilepsy portfolio** ([AKC CHF](https://www.akcchf.org/research/research-portfolio/program-area/epilepsy-research/)) — registry/funding, current, on-request — >$3M across 20+ grants; the directory names every investigator holding canine seizure data (e.g., Fiona James, U Guelph, video-EEG).
- **Canine Epilepsy Network / Epil-K9 (U Missouri)** ([site](http://www.canine-epilepsy.com/dnaresearch.php)) — registry/community, long-running, on-request — established epileptic-dog owner community; the practical recruitment channel for a future pilot with real seizure-positive households.
- **IVETF consensus reports** ([BMC collection](https://www.biomedcentral.com/collections/ivetf)) — standards, 2015, open — the standard veterinary vocabulary (generalized tonic-clonic vs focal vs paroxysmal non-epileptic). Aligning prompt and output terms to IVETF makes results legible to vets.
- **Pet Seizure Clinic, University of Glasgow** ([site](https://petseizureclinic.co.uk/short-posts/blog-post-title-two-4n7ga)) — clinic, current, on-request — dedicated veterinary seizure clinic whose wearables review confirms no current wearable is fully accurate; a named UK clinical partner candidate.
- **Nervus Ltd veterinary video-EEG** ([site](https://www.nervus.co.uk/veterinary-eeg)) — service, current, commercial — up to 72h wireless ambulatory video-EEG in freely moving dogs; a potential partnership source of time-synced video+EEG ground truth.
- **Continuous implanted EEG in epileptic dogs (Mayo/UMN)** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9631025/)) — paper/landscape, 2022, open paper (data closed) — Medtronic RC+S dogs recorded ~293 days each. Confirms the deepest canine seizure recordings exist but are implant-based and closed — ambient camera monitoring is an open niche.

## Recommendations for seizureGuard

Ordered by value-per-effort for a solo developer, one dog, one home camera, small budget.

1. **Switch confidence to logprobs and enforce structured outputs** (hours). Request logprobs on the yes/no token and threshold on P("yes") instead of trusting the verbalized confidence field (unreliable per ConfBench and related 2025-2026 calibration work); enforce a JSON schema with an `observed_signs` array so every positive is auditable. Also enable low-detail image mode if not already used (~75% token cost cut per frame).
2. **Upgrade the prompt from bare yes/no to canine semiology questions** (a day). The UCLA study shows bare VLM classification caps around F1 0.76 while concept-grounded pipelines (S3, CDSD) reach ~0.96. Ask about specific IVETF/JVIM-vocabulary signs — paddling, tonic stiffening, jaw clonus, loss of posture, ictal drooling — VERA-style, then apply a small rule layer over the answers instead of the current OR over confidences. Add AnomalyRuler-style normality rules induced from your own dog's normal footage plus temporal smoothing to tame false positives.
3. **Cut API cost with a model tier swap** (hours). gpt-5-nano as the recall-focused screen, gpt-5-mini (or gpt-5.4-mini) to confirm positives — roughly 8x cheaper input than gpt-4.1-mini at the screening tier.
4. **Add a free local pose gate** (a weekend). Run SuperAnimal-Quadruped zero-shot (or fine-tune yolo26n-pose on Dog-Pose) on the sampled frames; compute limb oscillation-frequency features (clonic jerking sits around 2-6 Hz — a decades-validated clinical signal) and only escalate ambiguous events to the paid VLM. This is the VSViG/CDSD/AltumView pattern, doubles as a privacy mode, and pose dynamics is what generalizes across subjects per the 2026 literature.
5. **Sanity-check the core assumption before tuning further** (a day). Probe your VLM on K9-Bench-style canine tasks and on staged/synthetic abnormal-motion clips (the CVPR 2026 zero-shot VAD result shows synthetic anomalies are a valid substitute for real ones) — cheap to do and directly measures whether the model can even see what you're asking about. A/B test few-shot exemplar images carefully; they can increase false positives.
6. **Adopt honest evaluation discipline** (ongoing). Benchmark against Epi-Moni (74.3% sensitivity, 0.018 false alarms/day, 11s latency) and Whistle (18.6-22.1% — the floor); use subject-wise/leave-one-dog-out splits if data from more dogs ever arrives; treat all video-only labels as noisy per the inter-observer studies; target generalized tonic-clonic events first.
7. **If training anything local**: pretrain on RodEpil (+ Animal Kingdom action classes), few-shot fine-tune on your own captured events following the Mar 2026 cross-species transfer recipe, and mine hard negatives (play, scratching, shaking off water) from DogMo, CREMD, and SmartHome-Bench.
8. **Watch list**: WildDog-Videos public release, NeuroMM-2026 challenge outputs, S3 access terms, code for the cross-species forecasting paper, and any published validation of Furbo/PetPace seizure features (none exists as of Aug 2026).
